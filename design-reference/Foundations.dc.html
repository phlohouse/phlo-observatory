Principles

01
Colour is a signal, not decorationMost of the screen is grey. Red, amber and green only appear when something needs you.
02
Say what happened, then what to doEvery problem shows its cause, its reach downstream, and the next action.
03
Problems first, healthy foldedAt 100+ pipelines, lists open on what's wrong and group what's fine.
04
Names stay exactTables, columns and IDs are always mono and never shortened mid-word.
05
Prod never looks like stagingAnything that isn't prod carries the amber stripe and pill.
